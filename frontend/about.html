Our Story

We're passionate about connecting people with great coffee through technology.

Where It All Began

Smart Café was founded in 2024 with a simple idea: great coffee shouldn't require waiting in line. We wanted to combine the warmth of a neighborhood café with the convenience of modern technology.

What started as a small coffee cart has grown into a beloved local spot where customers can order ahead, reserve their favorite table, and enjoy real-time order updates — all from their phone.
